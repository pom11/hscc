HSCC apps 1.4.2

Added

  • macOS: choose how often desktop widgets refresh. Settings → Desktop Widgets → Refresh every 1, 2, 5 or 15 minutes, or System (about 30 minutes). While the menu bar app runs it reloads every desktop widget on that cadence (default 5 minutes). macOS may still delay reloads to save energy; each widget shows how old its data is.

iOS is unchanged apart from the version number.
