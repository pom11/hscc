- **README gained a "Pausing a node" section.** The feature table had the
  one-liner and `docs/ops/node-pause.md` had the reference, with nothing in
  between: an operator who wanted to free one box for a training run had to
  open a 400-line doc to learn the dry-run default. The new section sits with
  the other ops sections and states the workflow (`pause` plans, `--apply`
  mutates, `resume --apply` restores what was running and clears the pause plus
  that node's heal strikes, `paused` lists what is parked, `--until` hands the
  deadline to the daemon's expiry tick), the one-model-on-one-node rule with its
  three no-override refusals (2+ units colocated, a unit that runs on other
  nodes, nothing to pause), the orchestrator-node guard as the ONE refusal
  `--force` unlocks, and the byte-identical promise — a pause is a separate
  durable overlay the repair paths consult, never an edit to templates,
  `serving.json`, the applied-template record, kanban caps, autodown or proxy
  config.
