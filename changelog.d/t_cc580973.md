  three escalated repairs logged no kill/reap line.** New section
  `Question (b) — escalation forensics` (b5-b10) appended to

  the days the sparkrun CLI was reachable (10-27: 2, 10-29: 13, 10-30: **0**

  broken — it is **blind, and silent about it**. Escalation #2 is closed by
  contradiction: its own `proxy start` printed `already running (PID 46269)`

  a failed status read. Escalation #3 **succeeded** — wrapper 91693 and its
