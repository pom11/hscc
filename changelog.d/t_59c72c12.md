- **A config-side non-OK step no longer records the template just because a
  launch step ALSO warned (t_59c72c12).** #842 lifted the applied-record veto
  for non-OK LAUNCH steps and stated the invariant that a non-OK CONFIG-side
  step "keeps the full veto" — but the code only filtered `unconfirmed` to
  launch steps, so with e.g. `proxy-declaration` warn + `provision` warn the
  launch warn alone set `launch_unconfirmed`, and the record was then written
  regardless of why `success` was False. The file's meaning — "these files are
  on the nodes" — was asserted while a config write had not landed, and the
  flag (`launch_unconfirmed: ["provision"]`) actively misattributed the gap to
  the launch. Now the waiver is granted only when EVERY non-OK step is a
  launch step; any config-side non-OK step anywhere reasserts the full veto and
  nothing is recorded. The launch-only waiver #842 added is unchanged.
  Reachable combination today: `proxy-declaration` warn + `provision` warn.
