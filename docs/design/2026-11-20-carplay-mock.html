The cluster on the car screen

You open HSCC on the car display and see everything on one screen: cluster health, boards, running work, what needs you, and the merge queue. Below are six designs drawn on a real CarPlay canvas (800×480 pt with the system sidebar), each with what it shows, how it updates and what Apple allows.

What Apple allows in CarPlay
  • CarPlay apps are built from Apple’s templates (information, list, grid, tab bar). Free-form drawing, like the widgets, is only allowed for navigation apps. Every design here uses real templates; numbers and gauges become rendered images inside template rows, which the templates allow.
  • A CarPlay app needs an Apple-granted CarPlay entitlement in a category (audio, communication, navigation, EV charging, parking, food ordering, fueling, driving task). A cluster monitor fits none of them, so expect Apple to refuse it. It still runs in Xcode’s CarPlay Simulator for development.
  • Design F needs no entitlement: since iOS 26, Live Activities appear on the CarPlay screen. It reuses the Cluster watch Live Activity you already picked.
  • While the car is connected the app is running, so a CarPlay screen can update every 30 seconds. The widget refresh budget does not apply.
