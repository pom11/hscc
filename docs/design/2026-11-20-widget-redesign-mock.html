Refresh budget.Every widget asks iOS to reload every 5 minutes, which is 288 reloads a day per widget. iOS gives a home-screen widget roughly 40–70 reloads a day and quietly drops the rest, so in practice tiles update every 20–40 minutes while still looking current. The proposal below sets realistic intervals, shows an “as of” time on every widget, greys out data older than twice the interval, and lets the app push a reload when it opens. The running audit (card t_1c4deed3) measures what actually happens.
